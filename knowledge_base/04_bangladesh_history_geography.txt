BANGLADESH HISTORY, LIBERATION WAR & GEOGRAPHY

1. 1971 LIBERATION WAR (MUKTIJUDDHO)
- Historical Context: 9-month armed liberation struggle against Pakistan.
- March 7, 1971: Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman delivers the historic address at the Racecourse Ground ("Ebarer shongram amader muktir shongram, ebarer shongram shadhinotar shongram"), inscribed in UNESCO Memory of the World Register.
- March 25, 1971: Operation Searchlight genocide launched by Pakistani military.
- March 26, 1971: Declaration of Independence (National Independence Day).
- April 17, 1971: Oath-taking of the provisional Mujibnagar Government in Meherpur.
- 11 War Sectors: Guerilla and conventional warfare organized across 11 designated sectors commanded by sector commanders.
- 7 Bir Sreshtho (Highest Gallantry Heroes): Captain Mohiuddin Jahangir, Lance Naik Munshi Abdur Rouf, Sepoy Hamidur Rahman, Engine Room Artificer Mohammad Ruhul Amin, Flight Lieutenant Matiur Rahman, Sepoy Mostafa Kamal, Lance Naik Noor Mohammad Sheikh.
- December 16, 1971: Unconditional surrender of 93,000 Pakistani troops at Dhaka (Victory Day / Bijoy Dibos).

2. 1952 LANGUAGE MOVEMENT (BHASHA ANDOLON)
- February 21, 1952: Students and citizens protested against Urdu imposition; police opened fire near Dhaka University.
- Martyrs: Salam, Barkat, Rafiq, Jabbar, Shafiur, and others.
- Global Recognition: In 1999, UNESCO declared February 21 as International Mother Language Day.

3. GEOGRAPHY & NATURAL ASSETS
- Capital: Dhaka. Currency: Bangladeshi Taka (BDT / ৳).
- Administrative Divisions (8): Dhaka, Chattogram, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barishal, Sylhet, Rangpur, Mymensingh (64 Districts).
- Major Rivers: Padma, Meghna, Jamuna, Brahmaputra, Karnaphuli, Surma.
- Sundarbans: Largest contiguous mangrove forest in the world, home to the Royal Bengal Tiger.
- Cox's Bazar: Longest unbroken natural sandy sea beach in the world (120 km).
